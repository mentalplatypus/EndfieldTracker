      A fan-made tracker, not affiliated with Hypergryph or GRYPHLINE. In-game times are server time (Asia UTC+8, Americas / Europe UTC−5) and are shown here in your own time zone. Version numbers are counted from launch (1.0). Until the next update is announced, its date is taken from when the current version's last events end, or else six weeks after the current one began.
