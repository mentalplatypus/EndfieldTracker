# Endfield Tracker

**Live:** https://mentalplatypus.github.io/EndfieldTracker/

One page that shows what's happening in Arknights: Endfield right now, without opening the game. It covers the current version and when the next one arrives, redeem codes that still work, events running now and coming up, headhunting and arsenal banners, endgame seasons such as Echoes of War, Umbral Monument and Contingency Contract, livestreams, and a calendar of every time-limited activity. Dates come from the official update notes, checked every three hours, and are shown in your own time zone for the Asia or Americas / Europe server. You can also add upcoming dates to your calendar.

A fan-made project, not affiliated with Hypergryph or GRYPHLINE.

Made by [David Wang](https://mentalplatypus.github.io/).
